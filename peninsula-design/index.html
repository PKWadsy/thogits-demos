About us

Local to the Hibiscus Coast for 25+ years

Peninsula Copy & Design has been running for 25+ years on the Hibiscus Coast, originally starting as Copy Cottage in a 'Cottage'. We have been at our current location for over 17 years.

We have the experience and equipment to print, copy, scan and design anything that you require, and we're committed to delivering quality, competitively priced work. Our staff are friendly and helpful, so contact us anytime to discuss your printing needs.
